Research

Structural Models

Writing Sample 1. My demand paper studies a misrecorded binary variable using discrete-choice models, linear programming, and moment inequalities. It develops partial-identification bounds and applies them to retail scanner data. Xiaoxia Shi provided guidance on econometric methods, while Panle Jia Barwick provided guidance on the industrial organization aspects of the project.

My project with Sha Yang studies follow-up automobile reviews, how owners update their evaluations after use, and what buyers can learn from those reviews.

AI in Econ and Marketing

Machine Learning for Decision Making

Writing Sample 2. My senior thesis with Kohei Yata studies personalized credit offers under fairness constraints. An earlier ECON 726 project uses multi-armed bandits to study sequential decisions and offline evaluation.

NLP for Economic Analysis

In my work with Kyeongbin (KB) Kim, I build news-text datasets and use NLP to study data-center media coverage, while helping develop a research design linking coverage to consumer perceptions and purchasing. With Sha Yang, I use language models to measure the content of automobile reviews.
